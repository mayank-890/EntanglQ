ENCRYPTION PROCESS
1. Message converted to ASCII bytes.
2. Each byte XOR'd with a quantum key segment.
3. Ciphertext transmitted over classical channel.

WHY EVE FAILS

ℹ️ QUANTUM ENTANGLEMENT — HOW IT SECURES DATA
1. SUPERPOSITION
Qubits exist simultaneously as |0⟩ and |1⟩ until measured — invisible to classical eavesdroppers.
2. ENTANGLEMENT
Measuring one qubit instantly collapses its partner's state — the foundation of quantum key distribution.
3. NO-CLONING
Copying an unknown qubit disturbs it — Eve's tampering is always detectable via elevated QBER.
